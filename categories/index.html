Module Design Overview



The module designs a comprehensive educational curriculum on adversarial machine learning security for AI systems in image and audio domains. It includes 15 modules and 50+ labs/projects covering digital and physical attacks such as adversarial examples and backdoor attacks targeting image classification, object detection, speech recognition, and voice assistants. Students learn both attack techniques and defense strategies, including adversarial detection, robust training, and secure system design. The program integrates hands-on labs, programming assignments, and training projects, and disseminates materials through initiatives like NSF SFS, GenCyber, and online platforms to train future engineers to build trustworthy and secure AI systems.
