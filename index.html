Platform Information

This project develops a comprehensive educational program on securing AI systems against adversarial machine learning attacks, focusing on both image and audio domains. It addresses the lack of AI security education by creating 15 instructional modules and 50+ hands-on labs/projects covering adversarial examples, backdoor attacks, and defense techniques. Students learn how attacks target both training and inference phases and how to defend systems using detection, robust training, and certified defenses. The program aims to train future engineers to design trustworthy, secure AI systems used in applications like autonomous vehicles, voice assistants, and surveillance.
